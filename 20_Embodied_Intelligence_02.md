## 1. The human brain's abilities

The human brain is actually very powerful. It constantly performs highly accurate 3D modeling, unlike a GPU. It has dedicated circuits and brain areas for this work, so performance is very high.

Humans also have strong attention. We can see details and the whole picture at once, with very long context. The brain almost never crashes. It quickly corrects unusual situations and is very stable. Manufacturing costs are very low. Food, water, and air are basically enough to stay alive. Humans also have built-in circulation and filtration, maintain themselves, move, understand human commands, and cooperate well locally with other humans.

The brain also generalizes well. Vision, hearing, touch, and final actions have all been refined well. The brain is three-dimensional, unlike the linear structure of traditional chips. A 3D structure can naturally use its physical shape to perform functions.

## 2. Advantages of biological computing

For example, suppose we want to compare the square root of two with the integer one. Nerves can directly build a diagonal and judge their sizes from the difference in signal travel time. A traditional computer can only calculate the square root of two or simplify it using mathematics.

People have already tested tiny pieces of human brain tissue in dishes. They have shown amazing ability, especially in learning. So biological computers have natural advantages. This is also the best choice produced by evolution.

Humans can also pass on knowledge. One individual has limited ability, but a group has enormous power. Humans are currently the strongest creatures on Earth. There is also information showing that humans continue to evolve and adapt better to modern life. They can no longer be compared to ape-like humans on the grasslands.
