## 1. Art style

The art style mainly uses anime girls. The character designs mainly take inspiration from xxx. There are no male characters.

The game uses 3D graphics with a 2D look. The setting mainly has a medieval style in another world, with a scientific system that developed from magic.

I think this direction has the best chance of giving the project a clear identity.

## 2. Hero appearance

Different heroes do not need to look very different from each other.

Players can freely change details such as hair style, hair color, and faces. This leaves enough room for personal choices within one shared world.

## 3. Armor heroes

One hero type is called "Armor." When its armor is damaged, an ordinary character does not appear inside. It is more like a tank or a large unit.

Different Armor heroes can look very different. They have high health but only average attack power. Their movements are more noticeable and give enemies enough time and space to dodge. Their role is similar to a boss.

So the teams are really fighting around several bosses.

## 4. Team size in standard mode

In standard mode, each team has 8 players, including at most 3 Armor heroes.

I thought about this number for a long time. On an Overwatch map, for example, 6 players can cover the terrain fairly well. They can form a clear front line without leaving obvious blind spots.

With only 5 players, the two teams may often fight intense battles, keep moving around, and attack from behind. This would not look very good.

I prefer battles with clear front lines. With 8 players, 5 can fight at the front, while 1 and 2 take the sides. Changes can happen on both sides, making tactics much more flexible and less predictable.

## 5. Parties and room for mistakes

More players also leave more room for mistakes. One or two players, or a regular small party, will have less power to deliberately ruin a match.

Standard mode allows parties of up to 3 players. Larger parties enter large-party mode.

Large-party mode does not use the players' "xxx." It only matches them with other large parties. A party may need all 8 players before it can start. Combinations such as 3+5 or 4+4 are also possible.

## 6. Hero selection limits

A team can choose at most 3 of the same hero. Heroes with special tags, such as portal heroes, are limited to 1.

The game has no traditional healer units. Heroes can quickly recover health on their own. Some heroes can use health potions, and some can pick up health packs. This lets more players stay in combat.

## 7. Game performance

Performance is extremely important. We will focus on the experience on low-end computers. We hope to reach 120Hz on low-end devices and 300Hz on mid-range and high-end devices.

The project does not use too many complex visual effects, so this goal is not completely out of reach.

## 8. Skill combinations

Skills should work together in more ways.

For example, a speed boost can increase the movement speed of some melee units. A hero with a charge skill can also stay fully charged under certain conditions.

## 9. Terrain that can be destroyed or built

Maps will have many walls and small boxes that can be destroyed, but they should not be too easy to destroy.

Some heroes can build walls. They can make cover about half a hero's height to reduce the chance of being hit. Other heroes can create vertical walls.

These walls usually stay permanently, but the skills have long cooldowns. The walls must fit the map grid exactly. Some hero skills also deal extra damage to certain wall types.

A map can have a good flanking route that is blocked by a wall at the start. The team must first destroy and remove that wall so that flankers can pass through.

Another map can have a bridge with an energy crystal under it. When the crystal is destroyed, the whole bridge falls down. This greatly slows the defenders' return to defend.

## 10. Ultimate energy

Damaging or healing heroes does not generate ultimate energy, except for a few heroes.

## 11. Aiming mode

Some heroes can have a built-in aiming mode. All calculations happen locally, and players can customize it in detail. This is especially suitable for heroes that shoot from a distance.

## 12. Hidden enemy health

Enemy health is always hidden. This is meant to avoid the very negative experience of being instantly killed by focused fire.

## 13. Map editor

The game has a map editor mode. Players can edit maps inside the game, with several editing levels.

At the most basic level, players can add, remove, change, and inspect things on an existing map. For example, they can add an obstacle, close a door, or add a health pack location. These actions are fairly easy to support.

## 14. Detection alerts

If a scouting device detects a player, that player should receive a clear alert telling them that their position has been revealed.

## 15. Mana

At first I considered adding mana, or a blue bar. Later I felt that this would make the system more complex.

So only a very small number of heroes may have mana. For custom heroes, the designer can choose whether to include it.

## 16. Avoiding traditional map changes

I strongly dislike traditional map changes, such as choosing one of three maps. They make stored energy disappear and break the flow of the game.

## 17. Payments and progression

The game is not pay-to-win. It also has no level progression after a match.

Skins are bought only on a monthly basis. There are no limited skins that can only be bought during a certain season.

The project does not have many art assets, but we can make some extra effects to earn money.

## 18. Crystal mechanics

Maps can also have crystal mechanics similar to breaking walls. When a hero activates a crystal, a door opens. Flankers can then move behind the enemy and activate other crystals. All heroes can activate these crystals.

This mechanic is also useful in campaign mode.

## 19. Match length

The length of a match is not fully decided yet. It should be around 5 to 10 minutes. In any case, it should not take too long.

## 20. xxx and match results

xxx only goes up or down based on the result of the whole match. No performance within a match counts toward the long-term score.

This means players cannot use a particular hero to gain points by exploiting the rating system.

## 21. ELO

An ELO system is necessary. We could even say that ELO is the core, while xxx is a supporting long-term measure.

## 22. New players and low ranks

New players and low-ranked players need special care. Deliberately losing rank and smurfing are forbidden.

Low ranks do not need their own completely closed area. The system only identifies highly skilled players for management.

Highly skilled players can also choose to play with low-ranked players or move to a lower rank. We can also consider separate quick-play and ranked pools.

If low-ranked players have enough highly skilled players around them, this can reduce smurfing and help new players learn some skills.

## 23. Match throwers and reports

The idea of a "match thrower" is vague and difficult to handle. We can only try to reduce the number of harmful players. We cannot promise to remove them all, so players should be encouraged to use reports.

## 24. Bases and respawn protection

The base is not an invincible area. It only gives fast health recovery.

Players receive 3 seconds of invincibility when they respawn.

## 25. Limits on forced control and terrain interference

"xxx" is forbidden, as are heroes that can forcibly pull teammates around. Heroes like Mei, who can create obstacles and change large areas of terrain, should not be in the game either.

## 26. Hidden match statistics

The game does not show players' scores, damage, or other statistics during a match. This avoids putting extra pressure on players.

But we can create an "all-out" state with three levels, each with different effects.

## 27. Match results, attacking, and defending

The result is not based on capture progress. It only depends on whether the objective is fully captured.

I also dislike switching attack and defense, because this makes matches much longer. Win or lose, players should leave directly when the match ends. Those who want to keep playing can start another match.

There is no need to make the teams switch sides and then compare them to decide the final winner. Just clearly show that the game is over.

## 28. A copying hero

Special skills are a complex subject. The game can have a hero who copies other heroes, with a lasting copy effect.

Each time this hero respawns, they can choose a teammate to copy again. However, they cannot use some of that hero's special skills.

If they copy an Armor hero, their health can also be reduced a little.

## 29. Hero selection and balance

Players cannot change heroes during a match, but the gameplay itself should offer more variety.

If players choose heroes before the match, the system can use win rates to help balance the two sides. This lets the game start faster. Defenders can also teleport directly to the front line, so combat can begin xxx seconds after loading finishes.

## 30. Point capture mode

Maps can also have a point capture mode. There are about 3 to 4 points at the start. At regular times, the game randomly chooses two of them as active objectives.

After each round, wait about 30 seconds before the next round. Capturing a point gives 1 point.

## 31. Preparation time and replacements

Voting at the start may take time, so the breaks and preparation stages should not be too long.

When a player leaves, the system should quickly find a computer player of a suitable skill level to fill the empty place.

## 32. Automatic management of harmful players

The system can use APM and performance data to decide whether to kick a player automatically.

Players should also be able to immediately mute or kick someone in the current match. However, this should require a high reporting threshold, and that person must have received repeated reports recently.

## 33. A support hero

There can be a support hero who gains charge when several nearby teammates die. They then turn into a combat unit for a short time, creating a small burst of power.

## 34. Grabbing and movement

One hero can throw xxx. After it hits a target, pressing the key again pulls the grabbed hero a certain distance.

## 35. Summoning skills

Several heroes can have summoning skills. This makes the whole battlefield more intense and puts more emphasis on coordination in small areas.

For example, the team can mark a location in advance, then press a key to summon a few monsters. The monsters can use fairly simple AI decisions.

## 36. Artillery skills

One hero can deploy a xxx turret and turn collected health packs into shells to load into it.

They can also use binoculars on the battlefield to call in artillery, firing three shells at once.

## 37. Terrain and map variants

Terrain can be more dramatic, with large changes. I like an underground maze art style, and the same style can be used for several maps.

One large map can also have several versions. For example, using spawn points A and B makes one map, while using C and B makes another.

## 38. Long guns and hitboxes

Long guns have no damage falloff. Headshots only deal 1.5 times the damage. The head's collision size can also be increased relative to the body.

The overall hitbox can be larger too. This reduces the mental pressure during design and makes the results of player actions more consistent.

## 39. Small hero tasks

Heroes should generally have small tasks. Some are easy and some are hard, and they are tied to hero talents.

However, these tasks should not change a hero too much over a match. Only a few tasks should bring a major change.

## 40. Campaigns and payloads

Campaign mode can include pushing a payload. Competitive mode may not need it, because a weaker attacking team may be unable to make any progress.

A campaign is better suited to a payload-like flow, completing objectives one by one.

## 41. Scene changes

Maps can also change during a match. For example, three minutes after the start, the central gate opens and both sides can enter the hall to keep fighting.

## 42. Combat presentation

Combat between heroes should look more impressive. Heroes should use more skills and depend less on their main weapons. There should not be too many melee heroes.

## 43. Rewards when behind

Death can also bring rewards. For example, a hero can gain ultimate charge after dying. This helps a losing team keep fighting.

## 44. Project progress

Development has started. The basic framework is already built, but there are no art assets yet.

If things go well, the first playable version may be ready before the end of the year.

I really do not have many options for the art, so please do not expect too much. Those who want to try it early can follow the project. I will post development updates and test versions from time to time.
